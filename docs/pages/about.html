Our mission

Make every AI agent accountable to the business

Inside-Out is more than a tagline. We start from what is actually running — the agents, tools and data in flight — and work outward to the controls, evidence and response.

Inside-Out · by default

See the live estate first

Inventories go stale in minutes. We keep a live, ownership-linked map of agents, MCP servers, tools and non-human identities across clouds.

Evidence over noise

Prioritize what is exploitable

Runtime context separates real exposure from theoretical risk, so teams fix the ~5% of findings that can actually reach production data.

Open by design

Built on kavach-mcp

Our detection engine grew out of an open-source MCP middleware. The rule families are public, portable and yours to extend.
