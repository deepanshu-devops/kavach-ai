Security controls

How the platform protects your data

Encryption

Protect

AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Customer-managed keys and BYOK available on enterprise plans.

Tenant isolation

Isolate

Logical isolation per tenant with per-customer data stores, namespaced keys and strictly scoped service identities.

Identity & access

Access

SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, role-based access control and mandatory MFA for all Kavach-AI personnel.

Secure SDLC

Build

Peer-reviewed changes, signed artifacts, dependency scanning and regular third-party penetration tests.

Monitoring & response

Detect

24/7 alerting on anomalous access, centralised immutable audit logs and a documented incident-response plan.

Resilience

Recover

Multi-AZ deployment, encrypted backups, tested restore procedures and a published uptime target.
