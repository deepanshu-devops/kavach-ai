The AI trust gap

There is no single leak. There is a chain of truths.

Agents are good at combining many individually permitted answers into something the enterprise never intended to share. A document here, a billing query there, a support transcript behind a third door — together they reconstruct business knowledge that was supposed to stay inside.

Kavach-AI catches exposure before it compounds: sensitive data is found and protected at the source, agent access is governed by policy at the gateway, and every inference is checked for leakage.

  • Source-first protection — data is secured before the model ever sees it
  • Gateway enforcement that runs the same way on every call
  • Continuous measurement of exposure and coverage gaps
